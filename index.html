When to come

The year, marked by waterపండుగలు

The flood brings the fish, the cold months bring the lamps and the chariots, and once in twelve years the whole state comes to bathe.

JanFebMarAprMayJunJulAugSepOctNovDec

LATE JAN – FEBMagha month

Antarvedi Kalyanam

Where the river meets the sea, Lakshmi Narasimha is married in the temple at Antarvedi. The next day a wooden chariot the height of a house is hauled through the town by thousands of hands.

26 JUN – 7 JUL 2027once in 12 years

Godavari Pushkaralu

Once in twelve years, when Jupiter enters Leo, a bath in the Godavari is held to wash the years clean, and millions come to the ghats between Rajahmundry and Kovvur to take it. The next twelve days begin on 26 June 2027.

JUL – SEPin flood

Pulasa season

The river runs red with silt and the hilsa swims up from the sea to spawn; caught here it is pulasa, the costliest fish in Andhra. Worth pawning your wedding gold for, the saying goes.

AUG – SEPten days

Ganesh Nimajjanam

Ten days of pandals end at the water, with drums, colour and Ganesha carried into the swollen river at Pushkar Ghat. Late August into September, depending on the moon.

NOVEMBERthe lamp month

Karthika Masam

A month of dips before dawn, and at dusk small lamps set afloat on the river, hundreds of flames on dark water. The month after Deepavali, which usually means November.

Good to know

Before you come

The practical answers, kept short.

Boat season

Papikondalu boats run for most of the year but come off the water when the river is in spate, usually for stretches of July to September. Check before you book, and take the morning departure.

Temple dress

Shoulders and knees covered; shoes off at the gate; phones away inside the sanctum. Trousers or a dhoti, not shorts. Prasadam is part of the visit.

Language

Telugu, in the accent the rest of Andhra calls the sweetest. English and Hindi work in hotels and on the boats. A “namaskaram” opens most doors.

Money

UPI works almost everywhere, small eateries included. Carry some cash for ferries, temple counters and roadside coconut. ATMs are easy in Rajahmundry and Amalapuram, scarcer in the villages.

If something goes wrong

Dial 112 for any emergency and 1363 for the national tourist helpline. Boats carry life jackets; wear one in the gorge and on the sea crossing. Hope Island has no shade or food until your boat returns. The big hospitals are in Rajahmundry and Kakinada.

Fly into Rajahmundry (RJA) or rail to Rajamahendravaram Junction — cross the river on the road-rail bridge for the best first look.

Getting here

By air

Rajahmundry Airport (RJA), 18 km out at Madhurapudi. Direct flights include Hyderabad, Bengaluru and Chennai.

By rail

Rajamahendravaram Junction, on the Howrah–Chennai main line — the train crosses the Godavari on the rail bridges as it arrives.

By road

On NH16: about 160 km from Vijayawada, 200 from Visakhapatnam. Dindi is 80 km south through the delta’s coconut lanes.

By boat

Papikondalu launches board upriver at the Gandi Pochamma Temple point — operators pick you up in Rajahmundry in the morning.

05:40

First light

Walk down before the town does. At first light the ghats belong to pilgrims, brass pots and mist — and the Godavari, wide as a sea, barely moving.

08:00

The temples at the water's edge

Uma Markandeyeswara. Kotilingeswara. Shrines the ghats have held for centuries, each a few steps from the water that made them.

12:30

An Andhra noon

Rice on a banana leaf. Gongura, avakaya, ghee. Eaten the way this district has always eaten — generously, and without hurry.

17:30

The river turns gold

Back to the steps as the light lowers. A slow country boat — or a speedboat, if you like your gold with spray.

19:00

Harathi

Fire returned to the water. Bells, flame, and the whole ghat singing to the river. This is what you came for.
